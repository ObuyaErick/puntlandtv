## Overview

This quotation covers the complete build of Puntland TV's digital service: a phone and tablet app for viewers, a private console your newsroom and control room use to run it, and the platform that carries live television, radio, news and catch-up video to both. Everything is delivered in Somali and English at equal quality, and is designed to work on the connections your audience actually has.

It also includes the two additions you have asked for: **live updates**, so that going on air, breaking news and newsroom changes appear instantly without anyone refreshing, and a **background work queue**, so that heavy jobs like video processing and sending notifications to every device run reliably without slowing the service down.

**Not included**

- Studio equipment, encoders and the studio's internet connection.
- Logos, brand guidelines and photography, which we assume you provide.

- Ongoing support after the ninety-day period, available separately on request.
