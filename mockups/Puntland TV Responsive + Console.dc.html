THE PLAYER-HEIGHT BUG, AND THE RULE THAT FIXES IT
Before — controls laid out inside the 16:9 box
Video height = width × 0.5625, so control space shrinks with device width. At 390dp it fitted; 360dp overflowed 12px; 320dp overflowed 34px. The most common Android width was the broken one.
After — three rules
1. The 16:9 surface owns video only. Controls overlay it and are measured against a 132dp reserved band, never against the remaining box height.
2. Below 360dp the transport row drops the skip buttons to icon-only 48dp and the quality/fullscreen pair collapses into one overflow button.
3. If reserved band < 132dp (landscape, or 320dp at 130% text) the player goes immersive: chrome hides, controls overlay the video, auto-dismiss at 3s.
Flutter: LayoutBuilder on the player shell, not MediaQuery.size — the shell is also used inside the expanded pane, where window width is not player width.
WHAT CHANGES, BY COMPONENT
Bottom sheet → dialog
At ≥600 the language picker and share sheet become a 400-wide centred dialog with a visible focus ring.
Full-screen editor → side panel
Console: at ≥840 the article editor opens as a 560-wide right panel over the list, keeping context.
Reading measure
Article body caps at 680dp ≈ 68 characters and centres. Hero image may bleed wider; text never does.
Pointer vs touch
Hover elevation, row hover fill #F4F6F9 and 2px #1D7EC0 focus rings render only when a pointer is present.
